How this manual works

This manual is universal knowledge — every team can read every section. The Roles section explains what each of the 8 archetypes does, including teams you're competing with. Knowing what your counterparties are optimizing for is part of the simulation.

Specific starting positions (cash, carbon allocations, legacy contracts) are private corporate information and live only in your team's encrypted briefing. If a number isn't in this manual, it's because it isn't yours to see.

The 8 roles

Eight archetypes × 3 variants = 24 teams in a full session. Each role has its own supply-chain function, trading partners, and event-vulnerability profile.

Brazilian Producer BR

Primary commercial Arabica origin (Santos port). Volume-driven, weather-exposed.

Goal: move volume profitably. Sells mostly to Trading Houses; some direct trades with Roaster Large.

Watch: Santos port disruptions (EVT-008), Cerrado drought (EVT-007), EUDR uncertified surcharge (EVT-010).

Ethiopian Producer ET

Specialty Arabica origin (Djibouti port). Lower volume, cup-quality premium.

Goal: defend the specialty premium against commercial Arabica. Buyers are Specialty Roasters and high-end Trading Houses.

Watch: Ethiopian export quota (EVT-013 caps exports at 50%), rust outbreak, Suez disruption (Djibouti route).

Trading House Alpha

Spread-capture intermediary. Buys from producers, sells to roasters.

Goal: margin between origin price and roaster sale price. Highest-volume archetype.

Watch: price shocks compress your spread; freight surges (EVT-009) hit your CIF margins.

Trading House Beta

Robusta-focused trading. Vietnam origin connections.

Goal: Robusta blend optimization, Asia-EU origin-to-roaster flow. Edge: information about Vietnam supply.

Watch: Vietnam typhoon (EVT-023, Robusta supply -30%), Suez Canal blockage (HCMC routes).

Roaster Large

Industrial-scale roasting. Stable blend procurement.

Goal: consistent blend at scale. Buys from Trading Houses primarily.

Watch: EUDR enforcement (uncertified Brazilian becomes expensive), Conscious Cup viral premium (EVT-005).

Roaster Specialty

Direct trade, single-origin focus. Higher margin, smaller volume.

Goal: certification-driven premium. Cup of Excellence bonus (EVT-018) rewards specialty roasters who buy specialty Arabica from Ethiopian producers in that round.

Watch: Verra crash (EVT-015 affects offset choices), Untraceable Offer (EVT-018 ethical dilemma).

Shipping & Logistics

Service role. Controls shipping rates + information about all flows.

Goal: margin on freight services + information broker advantage during disruptions.

Watch: Santos strike, Suez Canal block, freight surge — these are your moments. Pricing power is asymmetric.

Retailer / Brand

Downstream demand. Consumer-facing premium / mass-market.

Goal: shelf availability at target margin. ESG marketing is double-edged — Conscious Cup helps; greenwashing scandal hurts.

Watch: consumer demand shocks (EVT-017 retailer cancellations), institutional buyer surge (EVT-038 in R6).

Trading + VWAP

What can be traded

Three coffee grades + carbon credits:

  • arabica_commercial — baseline $4,409/MT (D001)
  • arabica_specialty — baseline $5,500/MT
  • standard_robusta — baseline $2,200/MT
  • Carbon offsets — Gold $50, Verra $25, Unverified $10 per tCO₂e

Lot size + max contract

Every contract is a multiple of 19.2 MT (one 20' FCL container — D002). Maximum per contract: 5 lots (96 MT). Same rule applies to forwards and options.

VWAP — Volume-Weighted Average Price

Per round, per grade: sum(qty × price) / sum(qty). The displayed market price needs at least 3 trades on that grade in that round to be valid (D015 ≥3-trade minimum). Below threshold, the system falls back to baseline (or event-shocked baseline — see Events).

Why a 3-trade minimum?

Prevents a single team from setting an artificial market price. With 24 teams trading aggressively, hitting 3 trades per grade is normally easy in any round.

Forward contracts

A forward = "I commit now to buy/sell X MT at Y price for delivery in round Z." Locked-in revenue/cost — you don't get the spot upside but you don't suffer the spot downside either.

Margin requirements

10% initial margin posted at write. 5% maintenance margin checked at end of each round. Mark-to-market: if spot moves against you, a margin call may require additional cash by next round or you face default.

Default penalties

Failure to deliver: $25K seller-side reputation penalty + $10K buyer-side. Reputation drops trigger -20% match priority next round (your trade requests get ignored).

Settlement

At the delivery round, a forward auto-converts to a physical trade at the contracted price. If spot has moved, the writer/holder may have made or lost money relative to the day's market — that's the point of forwards.

Options

Call = right to buy at strike price. Put = right to sell at strike price. Both pay a premium up-front (writer collects, holder pays).

At expiry

Intrinsic value:

  • Call: max(0, VWAP − strike) × qty
  • Put: max(0, strike − VWAP) × qty

If intrinsic > 0: exercise (writer pays holder the intrinsic value). If intrinsic ≤ 0: lapse (no further cash flow; premium stays with writer).

Strategy hint

Calls are bullish bets; puts are bearish. Selling premium is a yield strategy when you expect prices to stay range-bound. Buying premium is a cheap insurance policy if you expect a big move.

Carbon caps + offsets

Round-1 system cap (D006)

The cohort-wide R1 cap totals 1,105 tCO₂e, allocated across the 8 archetypes by industrial intensity (producers + roasters + shipping carry the largest shares; pure traders the smallest). Your team's specific R1 cap is in your encrypted briefing — it is private corporate information.

Cap tightening (D007)

Each subsequent round: 5% compound reduction. R2 cap = R1 × 0.95. R6 cap = R1 × 0.7738 (≈23% total reduction). Plan accordingly — buying offsets late is more expensive.

Overshoot penalty

$100/tCO₂e for every tonne over the cap (D006). Can be increased by carbon-event injections (e.g., EVT-006 binding caps, EVT-011 carbon tax +$30/tCO₂e on emissions above 80% of cap).

Min/max purchase per transaction

5 to 50 tCO₂e per transaction. To buy more than 50 in a round: split into multiple transactions.

D20 invalidation

At round close, every offset purchase rolls a 20-sided die. Threshold by tier:

  • Gold: roll 1 invalidates (5%)
  • Verra: roll 1-4 invalidates (20%)
  • Unverified: roll 1-8 invalidates (40%)

If invalidated, the offset's tons drop to zero for net-emissions calculation — but you still paid for it. Sunk cost. The cheapest offsets carry the highest expected loss.

The roll uses cryptographic randomness (crypto.getRandomValues); the result is deterministic for audit. Disputes are not negotiable.

Event cards (38 total — D039)

The deck:

  • 9 mandatory — auto-injected at round start. Cannot be skipped.
  • 8 herald — auto-embedded in the Herald newspaper. Narrative + foreshadowing.
  • 19 optional — facilitator chooses based on cohort dynamics.
  • 2 conditional — trigger only when behavior thresholds are met (e.g., greenwashing scandal if a team is heavily reliant on Unverified).

Effects propagate to live engines (D080):

  • price_shock — adds % or absolute deltas to baseline VWAP (e.g., EVT-008 Santos Strike +10% Arabica)
  • supply_disruption — drops origin capacity (e.g., EVT-008 Santos to 25%)
  • carbon_event — tightens cap or raises penalty (e.g., EVT-016 Carbon Emergency cap -8%)
  • logistics_delay — adds rounds to delivery (EVT-008 +1 round)
  • regulatory_change — surcharges at settlement (EVT-010 EUDR +$80/MT)

The round-tab "Active event modifiers" card always shows what's currently in force.

Settlement

End-of-round process:

  1. All confirmed spot trades for this round settle at the trade price (no MTM)
  2. All forwards with deliveryRound = current auto-settle as physical trades
  3. Open forwards (later delivery rounds) MTM against current VWAP — margin calls issued if applicable
  4. Carbon settlement per team: cap check, offset accounting, d20 invalidation rolls, penalties applied
  5. ESG scores updated (carbon performance + offset tier mix)
  6. Reputation status recomputed (green / amber / red based on overshoots + defaults)
  7. Round results broadcast to dashboards

Settlement runs on the server via BullMQ; typical duration ~30s. If settlement stalls beyond 2 min, facilitator escalates.

Composite scoring (40/20/25/15) — D012

Your final grade:

composite = 0.40 × in_game + 0.20 × peer + 0.25 × reflection + 0.15 × pre_post_growth

Critical insight: 60% of your grade is learning evidence (peer + reflection + pre/post), not in-game performance. A team can lose at trading and still place in the top tier on composite if their reflection + peer eval + knowledge growth show genuine learning.

Letter grade thresholds: A ≥ 90, B ≥ 75, C ≥ 60, D ≥ 50, F < 50.

In-game P&L (40%)

Role-normalized percentile: ((N − rank) / (N − 1)) × 100 where N = total teams. Top team = 100; last team = 0; symmetric distribution.

Sub-components inside the in-game score:

  • 50% Financial (final cash + inventory MTM minus starting cash)
  • 30% ESG (carbon performance + offset tier mix)
  • 20% Operational (deal volume, no defaults, on-time settlement)

Why role-normalized? Starting cash and carbon allocations differ by archetype. Comparing absolute P&L across roles would be unfair. The percentile rank is computed within your archetype-aware band.

ESG score

Composite of carbon performance + offset tier mix, clamped 0-100:

esgScore = baseScore + tierDelta
baseScore = (1 − totalNet/totalCap) × 100
tierDelta = Σ (tons_tier / 10) × tierEsgImpact

tierEsgImpact: Gold +2, Verra 0, Unverified -3 per 10 tCO₂e purchased. A team that buys 50 tCO₂e of Gold gets +10 ESG points; the same 50 tCO₂e of Unverified gets -15.

Reputation is a separate signal: green / amber / red, derived from overshoots + forward defaults.

Peer evaluation (20%)

Two parts:

  • Within-team (W1–W10): rate your own teammates 1–5 on Communication, Teamwork, Coordination, Support, Conflict Resolution, Motivation, Strategy, Digital Skills, Representation, Overall. Aggregated via trimmed mean (drop top + bottom if ≥4 reviewers) to discourage gaming.
  • Between-team (B1–B5): rate other teams you traded with on Fair Dealing, Clarity, Creativity, Reliability, Trust.

Open-ended comments matter. Anti-gaming: facilitators look for unusually high or low scores given by a single rater.

Reflection (25%)

5 prompts (R1–R5), choose at least 3 to address. Word count: 200–300 per prompt. Scored on the SOLO 5-dimension rubric (D058):

  • Depth — surface vs deep understanding
  • Evidence — citing specific simulation moments + data
  • Self-awareness — recognizing your decisions + biases
  • Transfer — connecting to real-world or career context
  • Clarity — coherent expression

Late penalty: -5 per 24h, max -15. Double-marking: 20% of submissions are graded by 2 facilitators with calibration tolerance ±10 points.

Pre/post knowledge (15%)

Same 20-MCQ quiz at start (pre) and end (post). 5 domains: Trading, Negotiation, ESG, Supply Chain, Digital. 30-min time limit.

gain = (post − pre) / max(1, 100 − pre)
prePostScore = gain × 85 + post × 0.15

85% of this component is your growth, 15% is your final knowledge level. A team that started at 30% and ended at 70% scores higher than one that started at 80% and ended at 90%. Learning is what we measure.

Sandbagging detection: if pre < 15%, time < 5 min, post > 70% → flagged for review. Don't try to fail the pre-quiz.

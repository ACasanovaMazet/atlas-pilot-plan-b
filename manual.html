How this manual works

This page is universal knowledge — every team can read every section. Your team briefing (linked from the Plan B portal landing) is encrypted and team-specific; this manual is the opposite: shared mechanics, no secrets.

Use the sticky table of contents on the left to jump between sections. The Roles section explains what each of the 8 archetypes does — including teams you're competing with. Knowing what your counterparties are optimizing for is part of the simulation.

The 8 roles

Eight archetypes × 3 variants = 24 teams in a full session. Each archetype shares supply-chain function and starting cash baseline; variants differ on cultural style, brand identity, and ±$200K cash deltas.

🇧🇷 Brazilian Producer

Supply-chain function: primary commercial Arabica origin (Santos port). Volume-driven, weather-exposed.

Starting cash: $800K baseline (variants ±$200K). R1 carbon cap: 210 tCO₂e (highest of any archetype, reflecting industrial-scale roasting + transport).

Goal: move volume profitably. You sell mostly to Trading Houses; some direct trades with Roaster Large. Your forward book may include FWD-001 (canonical: sell 100 MT to TH-Alpha @ $4,200, deliver R2).

Watch: Santos port disruptions (EVT-008), Cerrado drought (EVT-007), EUDR uncertified surcharge (EVT-010 +$80/MT).

🇪🇹 Ethiopian Producer

Supply-chain function: specialty Arabica origin (Djibouti port). Lower volume, cup-quality premium.

Starting cash: $500K baseline. R1 carbon cap: 80 tCO₂e (low; small-scale operations).

Goal: defend specialty premium ($5,500+/MT vs commercial $4,409). Your buyers are Specialty Roasters and high-end Trading Houses. Canonical forward FWD-002: sell 50 MT to Specialty Roaster @ $5,400, deliver R3.

Watch: Ethiopian export quota (EVT-013 — government caps exports at 50%), rust outbreak, Suez disruption (your Djibouti route).

Trading House Alpha

Supply-chain function: spread-capture intermediary. Buys from producers, sells to roasters.

Starting cash: $1.5M baseline (with $2.5M credit limit, the highest). R1 carbon cap: 70 tCO₂e (operational only, not transport).

Goal: margin between origin price and roaster sale price. You're the highest-volume archetype. Multiple legacy forwards on day one.

Watch: price shocks compress your spread; freight surges (EVT-009) hit your CIF margins.

Trading House Beta

Supply-chain function: Robusta-focused trading. Vietnam origin connections.

Starting cash: $1.5M baseline. R1 carbon cap: 85 tCO₂e.

Goal: Robusta blend optimization, Asia-EU origin-to-roaster flow. Your edge is information about Vietnam supply.

Watch: Vietnam typhoon (EVT-023 — Robusta supply -30%), Suez Canal blockage (your HCMC routes).

Roaster Large

Supply-chain function: industrial-scale roasting. Stable blend procurement.

Starting cash: $1.2M baseline. R1 carbon cap: 175 tCO₂e (high — energy-intensive roasting).

Goal: consistent blend at scale. You buy from Trading Houses primarily. Vertical integration variants (JDE, Nestlé) start with more cash.

Watch: EUDR enforcement (uncertified Brazilian becomes expensive), Conscious Cup viral premium (EVT-005 — certified demand jumps).

Roaster Specialty

Supply-chain function: direct trade, single-origin focus. Higher margin, smaller volume.

Starting cash: $700K baseline. R1 carbon cap: 70 tCO₂e.

Goal: certification-driven premium. Cup of Excellence bonus (EVT-018: +$50K to specialty roasters who buy specialty Arabica from Ethiopian producer that round).

Watch: Verra crash (EVT-015 affects offset choices), Untraceable Offer (EVT-018 ethical dilemma).

Shipping & Logistics

Supply-chain function: service role. Controls shipping rates + information about all flows.

Starting cash: $1.0M baseline. R1 carbon cap: 135 tCO₂e (high — transport emissions).

Goal: margin on freight services + information broker advantage during disruptions.

Watch: Santos strike, Suez Canal block, freight surge — these are your moments. Pricing power is asymmetric.

Retailer / Brand

Supply-chain function: downstream demand. Consumer-facing premium / mass-market.

Starting cash: $2.0M baseline (highest). R1 carbon cap: 280 tCO₂e (highest — full Scope 3 footprint).

Goal: shelf availability at target margin. ESG marketing is a double-edged sword (Conscious Cup helps; greenwashing scandal hurts).

Watch: consumer demand shocks (EVT-017 retailer cancellations), institutional buyer surge (EVT-038 in R6).

Trading + VWAP

What can be traded

Three coffee grades + carbon credits:

  • arabica_commercial — baseline $4,409/MT (D001)
  • arabica_specialty — baseline $5,500/MT
  • standard_robusta — baseline $2,200/MT
  • Carbon offsets (Gold $50, Verra $25, Unverified $10 per tCO₂e)

Lot size + max contract

Every contract must be a multiple of 19.2 MT (one 20' FCL container — D002). Maximum per contract: 5 lots (96 MT). For options + forwards the same rule applies.

VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price)

Per round, per grade. Formula: sum(qty × price) / sum(qty). The displayed market price needs at least 3 trades on that grade in that round to be valid (D015 ≥3-trade minimum). Below that threshold, the system falls back to the baseline (or the event-shocked baseline, see Events).

Why 3-trade minimum?

To prevent a single team from setting an artificial market price. With 24 teams trading aggressively, hitting 3 trades per grade is normally easy in any round.

Forward contracts

A forward = "I commit now to buy/sell X MT at Y price for delivery in round Z." Locked-in revenue/cost — you don't get the spot upside but you don't suffer the spot downside either.

Margin requirements

10% initial margin posted at write. 5% maintenance margin checked at end of each round. Mark-to-market: if the spot price moves against you, you may receive a margin call requiring additional cash by next round or face default.

Default penalties

If you fail to deliver on a forward: $25K seller-side reputation penalty + $10K buyer-side. Reputation drops trigger -20% match priority next round (your trade requests get ignored).

Settlement

At the delivery round, a forward auto-converts to a physical trade at the contracted price. If the spot has moved, the writer/holder may have made or lost money relative to the day's market — that's the point of forwards.

Options (calls + puts)

Call = right to buy at strike price. Put = right to sell at strike price. Both pay a premium up-front (writer collects, holder pays).

At expiry

Intrinsic value computed as:

  • Call: max(0, VWAP − strike) × qty
  • Put: max(0, strike − VWAP) × qty

If intrinsic > 0: exercise (writer pays holder the intrinsic value). If intrinsic ≤ 0: lapse (no further cash flow; premium stays with writer).

Strategy hint

Calls are bullish bets; puts are bearish. Selling premium is a yield strategy when you expect prices to stay range-bound. Buying premium is a cheap insurance policy if you expect a big move.

Cap tightening (D007)

Each subsequent round: 5% compound reduction. R2 cap = R1 × 0.95. R6 cap = R1 × 0.7738 (≈23% total reduction). Plan accordingly — buying offsets late is more expensive.

Overshoot penalty

$100/tCO₂e for every tonne over the cap (D006). Can be increased by carbon-event injections (e.g., EVT-006 binding caps, EVT-011 carbon tax +$30/tCO₂e on emissions above 80% of cap).

Min/max purchase per transaction

5 to 50 tCO₂e per transaction. To buy more than 50 in a round: split into multiple transactions.

D20 invalidation

At round close, every offset purchase rolls a 20-sided die (d20) for invalidation. The threshold depends on tier:

  • Gold: roll 1 invalidates (5% chance)
  • Verra: roll 1-4 invalidates (20% chance)
  • Unverified: roll 1-8 invalidates (40% chance)

If invalidated: the offset's tons drop to zero for net-emissions calculation, but you still paid for it. Sunk cost. The cheapest offsets carry the highest expected loss.

The roll uses cryptographic randomness (server-side crypto.getRandomValues); the result is deterministic for audit purposes. Disputes are not negotiable.

Event cards (38 total — D039)

The deck is composed of:

  • 9 mandatory — auto-injected at round start. Cannot be skipped (facilitator can deactivate but rarely will).
  • 8 herald — auto-embedded in the Herald newspaper. Narrative + foreshadowing.
  • 19 optional — facilitator chooses based on cohort dynamics.
  • 2 conditional — trigger only when behavior thresholds are met (e.g., greenwashing scandal if a team is heavily reliant on Unverified).

Event effects propagate to live engines (D080):

  • price_shock — adds % or absolute deltas to baseline VWAP (e.g., EVT-008 Santos Strike +10% Arabica)
  • supply_disruption — drops origin capacity (e.g., EVT-008 Santos to 25%)
  • carbon_event — tightens cap or raises penalty (e.g., EVT-016 Carbon Emergency cap -8%)
  • logistics_delay — adds rounds to delivery (EVT-008 +1 round)
  • regulatory_change — surcharges at settlement (EVT-010 EUDR +$80/MT)

Watch the round-tab "Active event modifiers" card — it always shows what's currently in force.

Settlement

End-of-round process:

  1. All confirmed spot trades for this round settle at the trade price (no MTM)
  2. All forwards with deliveryRound = current auto-settle as physical trades
  3. Open forwards (later delivery rounds) MTM against current VWAP — margin calls issued if applicable
  4. Carbon settlement per team: cap check, offset accounting, d20 invalidation rolls, penalties applied
  5. ESG scores updated (carbon performance + offset tier mix)
  6. Reputation status recomputed (green / amber / red based on overshoots + defaults)
  7. Round results broadcast to dashboards

Settlement runs on the server via BullMQ; typical duration ~30s. If settlement stalls beyond 2 min, facilitator escalates.

Composite scoring (40/20/25/15) — D012

Your final grade is computed as:

composite = 0.40 × in_game + 0.20 × peer + 0.25 × reflection + 0.15 × pre_post_growth

Critical insight: 60% of your grade is learning evidence (peer + reflection + pre/post), not in-game performance. A team can lose at trading and still place in the top tier on composite if their reflection + peer eval + knowledge growth show genuine learning.

Letter grade thresholds: A ≥ 90, B ≥ 75, C ≥ 60, D ≥ 50, F < 50.

In-game P&L (40%)

Role-normalized percentile, formula: ((N − rank) / (N − 1)) × 100 where N = total teams. Top team = 100; last team = 0; symmetric distribution.

Sub-components inside the in-game score:

  • 50% Financial (final cash + inventory MTM minus starting cash)
  • 30% ESG (carbon performance + offset tier mix)
  • 20% Operational (deal volume, no defaults, on-time settlement)

Why role-normalized? Your starting cash + cap differ by archetype. Comparing absolute P&L across roles would be unfair. The percentile rank is computed within your archetype-aware percentile band.

ESG score

Composite of carbon performance + offset tier mix, clamped 0-100:

esgScore = baseScore + tierDelta
baseScore = (1 − totalNet/totalCap) × 100
tierDelta = Σ (tons_tier / 10) × tierEsgImpact

tierEsgImpact: Gold +2, Verra 0, Unverified -3 per 10 tCO₂e purchased. So a team that buys 50 tCO₂e of Gold gets +10 ESG points; same 50 tCO₂e of Unverified gets -15.

Reputation is a separate signal: green / amber / red, derived from overshoots + forward defaults.

Peer evaluation (20%)

Two parts:

  • Within-team (W1-W10): rate your own teammates 1-5 on Communication, Teamwork, Coordination, Support, Conflict Resolution, Motivation, Strategy, Digital Skills, Representation, Overall. Aggregated via trimmed mean (drop top + bottom if ≥4 reviewers) to discourage gaming.
  • Between-team (B1-B5): rate other teams you traded with on Fair Dealing, Clarity, Creativity, Reliability, Trust.

Open-ended comments matter. Anti-gaming: facilitators look for unusually high or low scores given by a single rater.

Reflection (25%)

5 prompts (R1-R5), choose at least 3 to address. Word count: 200-300 per prompt. Scored on the SOLO 5-dimension rubric (D058):

  • Depth — surface vs deep understanding
  • Evidence — citing specific simulation moments + data
  • Self-awareness — recognizing your decisions + biases
  • Transfer — connecting to real-world or career context
  • Clarity — coherent expression

Late penalty: -5 per 24h, max -15. Double-marking: 20% of submissions are graded by 2 facilitators with calibration tolerance ±10 points.

Pre/post knowledge (15%)

Same 20-MCQ quiz at start (pre) and end (post). 5 domains: Trading, Negotiation, ESG, Supply Chain, Digital. 30-min time limit.

Score formula:

gain = (post − pre) / max(1, 100 − pre)
prePostScore = gain × 85 + post × 0.15

So 85% of this component is your growth, 15% is your final knowledge level. A team that started at 30% and ended at 70% scores higher than one that started at 80% and ended at 90% — learning is what we measure.

Sandbagging detection: if pre < 15%, time < 5 min, post > 70% → flagged for review. Don't try to fail the pre-quiz.
